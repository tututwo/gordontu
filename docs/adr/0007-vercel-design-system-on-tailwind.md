# Vercel's design system on Tailwind v4: Geist in Geist's roles, and square hairline chrome

The site's design system is Vercel's, from `docs/vercel-design-system`, run through Tailwind v4. `src/app.css` imports that folder's `theme.css` as Vercel wrote it (its colours, spacing, radii and shadows) and only Tailwind's utilities: no default theme, so Vercel's tokens are the only ones there are (`text-red-500` and `rounded-lg` do not exist), and no preflight, so the page keeps its own small base. Preflight's `display: block` on `svg`, `img` and `canvas` would pull the headline's inline Category link icons and avatar out of their lines, and its margin reset is replaced by one scoped to text blocks.

Type is Geist and Geist Mono (Fontsource's variable builds; Inter is gone), set in Geist's roles as Vercel's own brand stylesheet sizes them (vercel.com/geist/vercel-brand.css): `text-heading-48/40/32` tracked −0.06em, `-24` −0.04em, `-20` and `-16` −0.02em, all at weight 450, and `text-copy-18/16/14/13`, regular and untracked. Each `--text-*` token carries its leading, tracking and weight, so one class sets a whole role. They replace theme.css's own type scale, which is vercel.com's marketing sizes (30, 56 and 64px, tracked in px). Geist Mono only stamps, as DESIGN.md has it: `text-eyebrow` (its 11px uppercase label at 0.071em, for "Client", "Tools", a panel's heading, a form's labels) and Geist's `text-label-12-mono` for numbers and dates. Vercel's Geist feature settings are on `:root`. Where the landing's structure needs its own leading, scoped CSS overrides the role: the headline keeps a line-height of 2 for its icons, and the Category link's bar 1.3. Component styles are unlayered, so they win over the utilities layer. Postcard backs are drawn on a canvas, so `postcardBack.js` reads the same tokens and sets the same roles there.

Colour follows Vercel's greys: words in obsidian (#171717), with #000 kept for the drawings (the avatar, the icons, the brackets, the bio's pen stroke); stone (#666) for the grey bio, the tabs and every label; charcoal (#4d4d4d) for values under a label; ash (#c9c9c9) for the scramble; hairlines #ebebeb. The page stays pure white rather than theme.css's #fafafa canvas, because the lit card, the lens view of the afterword and the open postcard's caption hide what is under them by being the page's own white. The old tokens (`--paper`, `--ink`, `--muted-ink`, `--hairline`, `--font-sans`) are gone.

Corners are square and nothing floats on a shadow. That is Vercel's depth (a hairline, never a shadow), without its 6px radius, which was left out on purpose: the landing's rules, brackets and card outlines are all square. The gallery's frosted pills and rounded panels became white boxes with a hairline. The project index is now a list of rows between hairlines, like the landing's projects, under a heading that stays put. The rail's current section is a square of ink, like the bar across a lit Category link. The postcards keep their drop shadow, as content on a table: without it a white chart vanishes into the page.

Rejected: Tailwind's default theme under theme.css (its spacing multiplier makes `p-5` 20px beside theme.css's `p-4` of 4px, and its palette invites colour); preflight (above); rewriting every component in utility classes (type is set with the role classes; layout stays in each component's scoped CSS, with its reasons beside it); Vercel's wordmark and ▲ (they would say Vercel made the site).
