# Copy the uikit-expt card hover exactly, with three.js icons and a react-spring port

The landing is a white introduction whose headline names the three Project categories as Category links, and their hover is meant to be the card hover from uikit-expt.vercel.app, exactly, not something like it. That reference draws its whole card in WebGL with @react-three/uikit and animates it with react-spring, so we kept the parts whose look depends on WebGL and rebuilt the rest in the DOM. The icon is a three.js scene per link (three small canvases, loaded by dynamic import inside an attachment as in ADR-0002): white boxes inked by the reference's `fwidth` edge shader, fitted into the frame the way uikit's `<Content>` fits children, viewed orthographically. The brackets, the frame and the label's black bar are plain elements driven by CSS custom properties, so the text stays real, selectable text. Every value moves on a port of react-spring's integrator (`spring.js`, `config.default`, 1 ms steps) rather than GSAP or CSS transitions, because the reference's feel, including springing back mid-spin and unwinding, comes from that integrator and a tween cannot reproduce it.

We copied the **deployed** page (commit 6f34d27), not the repository's HEAD, because the deployed page is what was chosen and the two differ: Cubes at `transformScale` 1.1, edge strength 1.5, a faint 25%-opacity border, and a frame declared at 88px that the card's flex row squeezes to **70** while the brackets (8px arms, 2px bars, with the second bar overhanging to 10px), the 12→6 padding and the 0→−6 inset keep their sizes. The 70 was measured on the live page: the brackets spread 12 units = 24 device px on a 140 device px frame. Frame-by-frame screenshots of both pages were compared at the same scale. Some quirks were kept on purpose: the edge colour switches instantly (react-spring cannot tween a `THREE.Color`, so it jumps), and the shader writes linear colour values unconverted, which gives the edges their deep ink and violet. One quirk was dropped: re-hovering during the unwind loops from the current angle rather than jumping back to it every 5 s.

Rejected: CSS 3D boxes (visibly different line weight and seams at edge-on angles, and no `fwidth` hairlines), one shared canvas behind the page (scroll-synced scissoring for three icons that sit inline in text), and GSAP or anime.js for the hover (duration-based, and neither carries velocity across a retarget the way react-spring does). GSAP is used for the Bio revision, which is a timed sequence rather than a physical one. This supersedes ADR-0003.
